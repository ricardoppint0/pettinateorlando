Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Call Car Care Services handles information when you use the Call Car Care iOS app or send an appointment request.

Effective date
July 29, 2026
Applies to
Call Car Care iOS app and appointment requests
01

Information we collect

When you request an appointment, we may collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Contact details: your name and phone number. The iOS app does not collect an email address.
  • Service location: the address or location where you request mobile vehicle-care services.
  • Vehicle details: such as make, model, year, color, type, condition, and other information needed to understand the vehicle.
  • Appointment information: requested services, preferred date and time, alternative date, special instructions, and appointment communications.
  • Vehicle photos: images you choose to submit to show the vehicle’s condition, size, areas of concern, or requested work.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in vehicle photos or appointment notes.

02

How we use information

We use this information to review, schedule, confirm, and provide requested services; contact you about availability, pricing, service details, changes, or follow-up; locate the requested service address; understand the vehicle and requested work; maintain appropriate business records; respond to questions; resolve disputes; and protect customers and our business from misuse or fraud.

We do not use vehicle photos for advertising or public display unless we first obtain your permission.

03

How appointment information is sent and stored

The app prepares an SMS/MMS message addressed to Call Car Care Services at (407) 873-5020. You review the message and decide whether to send it using Apple Messages. The app does not send messages automatically.

Messages and attachments you send may be stored on your device, the business owner’s device, mobile-carrier systems, messaging-provider systems, and appropriate Call Car Care Services business records. Your carrier and messaging provider process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

04

When information is shared

Call Car Care Services does not sell your personal information. We may share information with workers or contractors who need it to fulfill your requested service; with providers that support communications or business operations; when required by law; to protect rights, safety, and property; or as part of a business transfer.

Service providers are expected to protect information consistent with their agreements and applicable law.

05

Access, correction, and deletion

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with your appointment by calling or texting (407) 873-5020. Describe your request and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant records. We may need to verify your identity.

We will respond within the time required by applicable law. Some information may be retained when reasonably necessary to complete a transaction, comply with legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce agreements.

06

Data retention

We retain customer, appointment, and vehicle information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining appropriate business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal or accounting obligations. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

07

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08

Children’s privacy

The Call Car Care app and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

09

Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as our practices, services, or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be posted here with a new effective date. If changes are material, we may provide additional notice through the app or another appropriate method.

10
